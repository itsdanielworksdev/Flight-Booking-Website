Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about booking, travel, and our services

How do I book a flight?

Simply enter your departure and destination cities, select your travel dates, choose from available flights, fill in passenger details, and complete payment. Your e-ticket will be sent to your email.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Yes, you can modify or cancel your booking through your account dashboard. Fees may apply depending on the fare type and timing of changes.

What baggage allowance is included?

Baggage allowance varies by airline and fare class. Economy typically includes 20-30kg checked baggage, while Business and First Class include 40-50kg.

How do I select my seat?

Seat selection is available during the booking process. You can choose your preferred seat from the interactive seat map before confirming your booking.

Is travel insurance included?

Travel insurance is optional and can be added during checkout. We partner with leading insurance providers to offer comprehensive coverage.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all major credit/debit cards, bank transfers, and digital wallets including Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and Apple Pay.
